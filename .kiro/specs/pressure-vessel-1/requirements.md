## Identity and learning objectives

- PVS-01: Challenge `pressure-vessel-1`, version 1, title Pressure Vessel I, difficulty intermediate, discipline Structures, modes Guided and Parametric (D-37: chosen before entry, fixed inside the workbench). Model `thinWallPressureVessel` version 1, a new id (MM-01). Catalog card `008`, art kind `vessel`, available (owner request, D-59). Storage key `ei:session:pressure-vessel-1:v1` with the v2 envelope (MM-18); design files `ei-design` schemaVersion 2 (MM-19).
- PVS-02: Learners discover, from tested results only, that (a) the hoop stress in a cylindrical shell is twice its longitudinal stress, so the hoop stress governs the shell; (b) membrane stress grows with radius and falls with wall thickness, so a wider vessel needs thicker walls; (c) a hemispherical head carries the same pressure at half the stress of a cylinder of equal radius and thickness, so the lightest acceptable heads are about half the shell thickness, not equal to it; (d) for a required volume, a wider, shorter vessel puts more of its volume in the efficient heads, which trades against the wall thickness it needs.

## Fixed values and adjustable design

- PVS-03 Fixed (locked): internal (gauge) pressure 4.0 MPa; hemispherical heads on both ends with the same inner radius as the shell; educational nominal steel (yield stress 250 MPa, density 7850 kg/m³); design factor 2.0 on yield, so the allowable membrane stress is 125 MPa (computed from the two fixed values, never typed separately). Locked fields: `materialId`, `pressurePa`, `designFactor`, `heads`.
- PVS-04 Adjustable (four parameters, all in millimetres in the UI): shell inner diameter 200–500 mm (10 mm steps); cylindrical shell length, excluding the heads, 100–1200 mm (10 mm steps); shell wall thickness 1–12 mm (0.5 mm steps); head wall thickness 1–12 mm (0.5 mm steps). The parameters are independent: every in-range combination is a buildable vessel. The step grid is the control grid; strict text edits accept any in-range value (existing clamp/strict semantics, MM-09).
- PVS-05 Initial design (baseline, D-47): inner diameter 240 mm, shell length 1200 mm, shell 2.5 mm, heads 1.5 mm. Internal reference (never shown to students): 400 mm, 530 mm, 6.5 mm, 3.5 mm. The reference is one acceptable design, not the only one.

## Metrics and checks

- PVS-06 Displayed metrics: hoop stress, longitudinal stress, head membrane stress (MPa, one decimal); internal volume (litres, one decimal); overall length (mm); mass (kg, two decimals); shell and head radius-to-thickness ratios (display only).
- PVS-07 Checks, in this order: `hoopStress` ≤ allowable, `longitudinalStress` ≤ allowable, `headStress` ≤ allowable, `volume` ≥ 100 L (0.100 m³), `overallLength` ≤ 1000 mm, `mass` ≤ 55 kg, `applicability` (bool). Upper limits pass iff `withinLimit(value, limit)`; the volume minimum passes iff `withinLimit(limit, value)` (inclusive, REL_TOL), and its utilization is limit ÷ value so that utilization ≤ 1 means pass for every check. Accepted iff applicable and every check passes (MM-11).
- PVS-08 Prediction: checkpoints on iterations 1 and 3; primary metric the hoop stress, named "hoop stress"; ratio band 0.98/1.02 (MM-13).
- PVS-09 Reflection prompt: "Using your tested designs, explain why the hoop stress governs the shell, how the diameter and the wall thicknesses changed the stresses, the volume and the mass, and why the heads did not need to be as thick as the shell."

## Applicability and exclusions

- PVS-10 Screens (inclusive on the safe side, REL_TOL, model constants never configuration): `THIN_WALL_SHELL` t_s ÷ r and `THIN_WALL_HEAD` t_h ÷ r, warning above 0.05 (r/t below 20) and not applicable above 0.1 (r/t below 10); `PLASTICITY` largest membrane stress ÷ yield, warning above 0.8 and not applicable above 1.0. A not-applicable design is never Accepted and its Status says so with the reason (clear message naming the thin-wall rule r/t ≥ 10).
- PVS-11 Always-listed unsupported effects: discontinuity (bending) stresses where the heads meet the shell; radial stress through the wall (thin-wall membrane theory); weld joint efficiency, corrosion allowance and manufacturing tolerances; openings, nozzles and their reinforcement; external pressure and buckling; cyclic pressure and fatigue; thermal stresses; supports, saddles, self-weight and the weight of the contents; brittle fracture and material toughness; pressure-vessel code rules (this is not a code calculation). Plasticity and thick walls are screened only.
- PVS-12 The heads' thickness step at the junction (head outer radius r + t_h differs from the shell's r + t_s) is drawn as modeled; the transition taper is not modeled and is part of the discontinuity exclusion.
