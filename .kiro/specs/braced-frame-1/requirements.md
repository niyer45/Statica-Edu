# Braced Frame I — scoped model requirements (task 49.1)

Additive specification, 2026-10-17. Refines `../original-catalog/` BF-01–BF-08 under the shared contracts in `../multi-model-architecture/` (MM-01–MM-27). Braced Frame I is fifth in the D-38 delivery order. Status: specification and independently computed worksheet only. The model is not implemented, the card stays unavailable until 49.3, and owner engineering review (29.2) of constants, equations, thresholds and wording is pending. No code is part of this task.

## Identity and learning objective

- BFR-01 Identity: challenge `braced-frame-1`, version 1, title Braced Frame I, advanced difficulty, Guided/Parametric modes. Model `pinJointedBracedFrame` version 1. The catalog copy (005 "Follow the forces through a frame and strengthen what matters.", concepts Frame analysis and Stability) can stay as written only if the learner-facing copy states the idealization. "Stability" here means the stability of a pin-jointed idealization (stable or mechanism) plus member buckling checked one member at a time. It does not mean global frame buckling or second-order stability. The challenge statement must contain this sentence, or an owner-approved equivalent: "This frame is modeled as pin-jointed: every joint is a hinge, so members carry only tension or compression. Without a diagonal, a storey of this idealized frame cannot resist sideways load. Real frames with welded or bolted joints have some bending resistance that this model ignores." Under OC-15, the owner must confirm the copy before activation.
- BFR-02 Objective: learners discover that (1) an unbraced pin-jointed storey is a mechanism; (2) sideways shear accumulates down the frame, so the lower diagonal and the leeward lower column carry the largest forces; (3) the direction of a diagonal decides which members are in tension and which are in compression, and long compression members are governed by buckling; (4) stiffness and strength are different things (the stable baseline drifts less than the reference but fails on buckling); (5) upsizing everything fails the mass limit, so only the governing members should be strengthened.

## Fixed values and adjustable design

- BFR-03 Fixed (locked): template `oneBayTwoStorey` with bay width 4.0 m and storey height 3.0 m, so each diagonal is 5.0 m. Joints are j1 (0, 0), j2 (4, 0), j3 (0, 3), j4 (4, 3), j5 (0, 6) and j6 (4, 6) m. Supports are pins at j1 and j2 with both translations fixed; they resist uplift, and anchorage is not checked. Members always present: columns `col1L` j1–j3, `col1R` j2–j4, `col2L` j3–j5 and `col2R` j4–j6, and beams `beam1` j3–j4 and `beam2` j5–j6. Candidate diagonals: `diag1a` j1–j4 and `diag1b` j2–j3 in storey 1, and `diag2a` j3–j6 and `diag2b` j4–j5 in storey 2. Diagonals that cross are not connected at the crossing. There is one static load case: 20 kN horizontal at j3 and 40 kN horizontal at j5, both in +x (left to right), plus 40 kN downward at j5 and at j6. Material is educational nominal steel: E 200 GPa, yield stress 250 MPa, density 7850 kg/m³. Self-weight is not applied. Locked fields: template/geometry, supports, loads, material, and the presence of columns and beams.
- BFR-04 Adjustable: (a) presence of each of the four candidate diagonals, toggled independently, so each storey can have none, a single diagonal in either direction, or X bracing; (b) the section of each of the 10 members, chosen per member from a four-entry catalog of circular hollow sections: `CHS48.3x3.2`, `CHS60.3x4.0`, `CHS76.1x4.0` and `CHS88.9x5.0` (outer diameter × wall in mm; A and I are derived, see design.md). Sizing is per member, not per group, so "strengthen what matters" is literal and the member table doubles as the control. Ten section selects plus four toggles fit a 320 px table. An absent diagonal keeps its section choice so that it is restored when re-enabled, but it contributes nothing to analysis or mass. Every combination is a valid design, mechanisms included, because learners must be able to test an unbraced storey (MM-09). There are no continuous inputs.
- BFR-05 Initial design: single diagonals `diag1b` and `diag2b`, each running from the windward upper corner down to the leeward lower corner of its storey, so both are compressed by the load. Every member is `CHS60.3x4.0`. The baseline is deliberately stable but failing, so that the iteration-1 prediction is scorable (MM-13). Mechanisms are discovered in a Guided step instead. Internal reference (never exposed to students): `diag1a` + `diag2a` with col1L `CHS48.3x3.2`, col1R `CHS88.9x5.0`, col2L `CHS60.3x4.0`, col2R `CHS76.1x4.0`, beam1, beam2 and diag1a `CHS76.1x4.0`, and diag2a `CHS48.3x3.2`. The reference is one of 618 accepted designs (design.md), not the only one.

## Metrics and constraints

- BFR-06 Displayed metrics: stability status (stable, or unstable mechanism). Roof drift: the mean horizontal displacement of j5 and j6 (mm, two decimals). Floor-1 drift: the mean of j3 and j4. Inter-storey drift ratio for each storey and the maximum, shown as a ratio and as "h/n". Each member's axial force (kN, tension positive), labeled Tension, Compression or ≈ 0. Each member's capacity, governing mode (tension yield, compression yield or Euler buckling) and utilization (%). Maximum utilization with its governing member. Mass (kg). Support reactions (kN, text). For a mechanism, only the stability status, mass and the list of storeys without a diagonal are shown. Displacements, forces, utilizations, drift and reactions are absent ("not defined: the frame is a mechanism"), never zero.
- BFR-07 Constraints, in order: `stability` (bool, must be stable), `roofDrift` ≤ 0.012 m (12 mm, 6 m/500), `utilization` ≤ 1.0 (ratio), `mass` ≤ 200 kg, `applicability` (bool). Inclusive comparisons use `withinLimit` with REL_TOL. For a mechanism, the `roofDrift` and `utilization` entries are absent (undefined, not failed), so the ordered constraints are `stability` (failed), `mass` and `applicability` (failed), with status `modelNotApplicable` (MM-10/MM-11). Margins: `massUsage` always; `driftUsage` and `maxUtilization` only when solved. Inter-storey drift ratio is a displayed metric, not a constraint: an exhaustive search found that a 1/400 limit would never be the only failed check (0 of 2,359,296 stable designs), so it would be redundant with `roofDrift`.
- BFR-08 Prediction: checkpoints on iterations 1 and 3. The primary metric is |roof drift|, named "roof drift" in the dialog. Ratio band 0.98/1.02, as for the beam. If either compared result is a mechanism, the outcome is not scorable (MM-13, OC-07).
- BFR-09 Reflection prompt: "Using your tested designs, explain how the diagonals changed the path the sideways load took to the supports, and why you strengthened some members but not others."

## Analysis, mechanism and applicability

- BFR-10 Analysis: 2D linear-elastic pin-jointed frame (truss idealization) with axial members only, solved by the direct stiffness method under small displacements, as specified in design.md. It is deterministic and returns only finite numbers. Tension is positive. Member forces are recovered from joint displacements, and reactions come from the full stiffness matrix. Compression capacity is the lesser of the squash load A·f_y and the pinned-end Euler load π²EI/L² (K = 1, full member length). Tension capacity is A·f_y. Utilization is |N|/capacity, with no resistance or safety factors (educational nominal). Forces above utilization 1 are nominal elastic values with no yielding or post-buckling redistribution, and the assumptions list says so.
- BFR-11 Mechanism: the reduced stiffness matrix K_ff (8 × 8) is factorized by a Cholesky factorization with diagonal pivoting. If a pivot is ≤ 1e-9 × the largest diagonal entry of K_ff, the frame is a mechanism. A solved system must also pass an equilibrium residual check, ‖K_ff u − P_f‖₂/‖P_f‖₂ ≤ 1e-9, or it is reported as a mechanism. A mechanism analysis has `status: 'mechanism'`, `applicable: false` and notApplicableReason `MECHANISM`, and it stores no displacements, forces, reactions, utilizations or drifts (OC-11, MM-10). Detection is numeric only, with no topology shortcut. Tests must show that it agrees with the topology rule "mechanism if and only if a storey has no diagonal" for all 16 diagonal combinations. Measured separation over every design: largest |pivot ratio| in mechanisms 8.4e-16, smallest in stable designs 1.52e-2.
- BFR-12 Screens (inclusive on the safe side, REL_TOL), in a frame-specific constants module:
  - `INELASTIC_BUCKLING`: for compression members with slenderness λ = L/r below λ_c = π√(2E/f_y) = 125.66, the metric is max |N|/N_J, where N_J = A f_y [1 − f_y λ²/(4π²E)] (Johnson parabola, used as a screen only and never as capacity). Warning above 0.8; not applicable above 1.0. It tells the learner that the Euler/squash capacity overestimates a stocky member's strength. In this catalog only `CHS76.1x4.0` and `CHS88.9x5.0` at 3 m have λ < λ_c. No otherwise-accepted design is made not applicable by this screen (exhaustive search), but `uniformS3Tension` warns.
  - `LARGE_DRIFT`: the metric is the maximum inter-storey drift ratio. Warning above 0.01; not applicable above 0.02 (small-displacement assumption). This is defense in depth: the largest drift ratio of any stable design in the catalog is 0.00339. Because loads are fixed, the P-Δ stability coefficient θ = PΔ/(Vh) is at most 2 × drift ratio = 0.0068 for every design, which justifies omitting P-Δ in this edition.
- BFR-13 Always-listed assumptions: pin joints, loads at joints only, linear elasticity, small displacements, straight prismatic members, rigid pinned supports that resist uplift, unconnected crossing diagonals, in-plane behavior with out-of-plane restraint at the joints, K = 1, nominal forces beyond capacity, and no self-weight. Always-listed unsupported effects: rigid or semi-rigid joints and frame bending action; P-Δ and second-order effects (bounded by the screen only); tension-only bracing and post-buckling redistribution; load reversal and other load cases (only one static load direction is analyzed, whereas real bracing must resist load from either side); connection design, eccentricity and bolt/weld capacity; member imperfections and residual stresses (inelastic buckling is screened only); out-of-plane and flexural-torsional buckling; local buckling of the tube walls (catalog D/t ≤ 19.0); dynamic, wind-gust and seismic response; self-weight, which is not configured in v1; and foundation flexibility and support settlement.
- BFR-14 Zero-force classification: a member with |N| ≤ 1e-9 × max|N| is labeled ≈ 0. Its utilization is |N|/(A f_y) whatever the sign of the round-off, so the result never depends on noise. A force is never displayed as "−0".

## Verification and integration

- BFR-15 Benchmarks: implement every worksheet row in design.md. Displacements, drift, mass and ratios must agree at 1e-9 relative. Forces and reactions must agree within 1e-9 × max|N| of the fixture, and utilizations within 1e-9 absolute. Also implement the independent checks (method of joints, virtual work, force method, global and joint equilibrium), the scaling and limiting cases, and the mechanism fixtures listed there. Invalid designs and configs are rejected before analysis.
- BFR-16 Presentation: a main-bundle 2D SVG frame view, which is also the fallback, with no Three.js dependency. It shows joints, supports, loads, present members and absent candidate diagonals (faint and dashed, labeled "not present"). Tension, compression and ≈ 0 are distinguished by line pattern plus a text label (T/C/0 and kN value), never by color alone. Member stroke width follows the section. An optional display-only deformed shape uses the stored displacements × a capped exaggeration factor, with the factor stated in text. Controls: the four diagonal toggles are buttons with `aria-pressed` and names such as "Storey 1 diagonal, bottom left to top right", and each member's section is chosen in an accessible member table that also lists force, sense, capacity, mode and utilization. Pointer selection in the SVG is optional and always mirrored by the table. For a mechanism, the view states "Unstable: storey N has no diagonal" in text and draws no displaced shape or forces. Prediction, history and compare use "roof drift", never deflection or beam labels (MM-14).
- BFR-17 Guided content (MM-21). Inspect: test the baseline and find the governing member, whether it is in tension or compression, and why. Experiment: remove one diagonal and predict, then observe the mechanism; restore it and flip a diagonal's direction, predicting the change in roof drift. Compare: compare two designs' governing members, roof drift and mass, and notice that the forces in a single-diagonal frame do not change when sections change. Explain: describe the load path with tested values and name one unmodeled effect, such as rigid joints or load reversal. Hints must not state the reference sections or invent numbers, and must not claim that one diagonal direction is always best.
- BFR-18 Gating: the card remains unavailable until 49.3 verifies the integrated learning loop on desktop and Pixel 7, including keyboard, fallback, 320 px and axe gates. Customer release additionally requires owner engineering review (29.2) and manual/student QA (OC-14).
